Waypoint 01 / 11

Introduction

Water Sort ("the app," "we," "us") is a liquid colour-sort puzzle in which you pour liquids between bottles until every bottle holds a single colour. The app has no user accounts and no sign‑in. Every level, your progress, and your coin and booster balances are generated and stored on your device — there is no game server and playing a level never requires the internet.

This Privacy Policy explains what the app stores on your device, what the Google Firebase services it uses for analytics, crash reports and notifications may collect, and the choices available to you. It applies to the app as distributed on Google Play and is effective as of 10 October 2026. By installing or using Water Sort, you agree to the practices described here.

Waypoint 02 / 11

Information we collect

The app asks for no personal information and runs no game server. It stores your progress only on your device, and the Google Firebase services it uses collect a limited set of technical and usage data automatically as part of their normal operation.

Game progress (on‑device)

Your current level, the board you are in the middle of, coins, lives, boosters, daily‑challenge streak, daily tasks, bonus‑chest progress, claimed Journey rewards and your card Collection. This is saved to a local file on your device and is never uploaded by us.

Your nickname & avatar (on‑device)

An optional display name and avatar you can set or change in your Profile. The nickname is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere — there's no account for it to be attached to.

Settings (on‑device)

Sound, music, vibration and notification switches, colour‑blind patterns, reduce motion and language. All saved locally so the app remembers your preferences between sessions. The on/off state of these switches is also recorded as usage data (see below).

Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

Which screens you open and how you play: levels started and finished, moves, undos and restarts, boosters used, tutorial steps, settings changed, and your player level and streak. Firebase also collects technical data automatically — device model, operating system, app version, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, a random app‑instance ID and, where available, your Advertising ID.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits an error, Crashlytics receives a report: the error and stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, whether the app was in the foreground, and a random installation ID. It contains no gameplay content and is used only to fix bugs.

Push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

Only if you turn Notifications on: the app registers a messaging token for your device and subscribes it to a general "all players" topic so we can send reminders and news. Opening a notification is recorded as a usage event. Turn it off any time in Settings.

What we do not collect

The app has no accounts or sign‑in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or calendar. It does not use the camera or microphone, and it does not access your photos or files. Your game progress and nickname are never transmitted to us. The app does not process real payments, so it never sees any card or banking details (see "Ads & purchases" below).

Waypoint 03 / 11

How we use information

  • Gameplay — generating levels, tracking your moves, and saving your progress so you can resume exactly where you left off, even after closing the app.
  • Rewards & timers — remembering your coins, boosters and lives, and working out when lives refill and daily tasks and challenges reset, from times stored on your device.
  • Profile & preferences — remembering your nickname, avatar and settings so the app behaves consistently.
  • Improving the game — using aggregated usage analytics to see where levels are too hard or too easy, which features are used, and what to fix or build next.
  • Stability — using crash reports to find and fix bugs.
  • Notifications — if you opt in, telling you about daily challenges, rewards and updates.

Waypoint 04 / 11

Third‑party services & data sharing

We don't sell your information and we don't operate a game backend. The app integrates three Google Firebase services for analytics, crash reporting and notifications. Each receives only the data it needs to do its job and handles it under Google's own policies.

Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Measures how the app is used so we can improve it. May process screen and gameplay events, device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, your Advertising ID and a random app‑instance ID. See Firebase privacy & security and Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase Crashlytics

Collects crash and error reports (stack traces, device model, OS and app version, a random installation ID) so we can fix problems. See Firebase privacy & security.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

Delivers push notifications through Google Play services. Uses a device messaging token and your opt‑in to the "all players" topic. See Firebase privacy & security.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.

Waypoint 05 / 11

App permissions

Water Sort's own gameplay needs no special permissions. The permissions in the app come from the notification feature and the Google Firebase SDKs. You can review app permissions at any time in Android Settings.

  • Internet & network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — used only by Firebase to send analytics, crash reports and notifications. Levels, saving and all game modes work fully offline.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — on Android 13 and newer, Android asks for your permission before the app can show notifications. The app asks once after your third win, or when you switch Notifications on in Settings. You can say no and keep playing.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and the Android Privacy Sandbox ad‑services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION) — added automatically by Firebase Analytics for measurement and attribution. The app shows no third‑party ads.
  • Push delivery (com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE, WAKE_LOCK) and the Google Play install‑referrer service — added by Firebase so notifications can arrive and installs can be measured.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, SMS, call log, storage/files, or location permissions.

Waypoint 06 / 11

Ads & purchases

In this version of Water Sort, ads and in‑game purchases are simulated inside the game. No ad network and no payment service is connected, so no ad data is collected and no real money changes hands.

  • Optional "watch a video" rewards — offered when you need an extra life or booster. They are a short placeholder countdown with a close button, always optional, and are not served by any ad network. The app does not show third‑party ads.
  • Shop — coin and booster bundles are credited inside the game after a confirmation step. You are never asked for payment details and you are never charged. The Shop and Settings pages show placeholders for restoring purchases.
  • Advertising ID — Firebase Analytics may read your Advertising ID for measurement. You can reset or delete it, or opt out of ads personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

If we add real ads or real purchases in the future, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent.

Waypoint 07 / 11

Storage, security & retention

Your progress, nickname, avatar, settings and in‑progress board are stored in local files in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Progress is saved after changes and when the app goes to the background, and a damaged save falls back to a fresh start rather than failing. There is no cloud account and no game database.

Device backup. We do not run any backup service. Android's own Auto Backup may include the app's local data in your Google account backup if backup is switched on in your device settings; that is managed by Android and Google, not by us.

Retention. Your local save stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Analytics, crash and messaging data handled by Firebase is retained under Google's own retention settings and policies. All data sent by the Firebase SDKs travels over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

Waypoint 08 / 11

Your rights & choices

Wherever you live, the app gives you direct control over what it stores. Depending on your region (for example, GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California) you may also have statutory rights to access or delete personal data held about you.

Delete your local data with Android Settings → Apps → Water Sort → Storage → Clear storage. This erases your progress, coins, boosters, nickname and settings from the device.

Change your nickname and avatar at any time from your Profile.

Turn notifications off with the Notifications switch in Settings, or in Android's app notification settings.

Limit advertising identifiers by resetting or deleting your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Adjust accessibility and feedback settings — colour‑blind patterns, reduce motion, sound, music and vibration — from Settings at any time.

Uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device.

For data the app stores on your device, the controls above are the fastest way to exercise your rights. For data held by Google (Firebase analytics, crash and messaging data), use Google's account and privacy tools, or contact us and we will help direct your request.

Waypoint 09 / 11

Children's privacy

Water Sort is a general‑audience puzzle game, is not directed at children under 13, and is not part of Google Play's "Designed for Families" programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will take appropriate action.
